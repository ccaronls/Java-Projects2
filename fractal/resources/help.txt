Supported functions:
--------------------
sin, sinh, asin, asinh
cos, cosh, acos, acosh
tan, tanh, atan, atanh

E  (Euler's)
PI, PHI (Golden Ratio)
X  Real(Zi)
Y  Imag(Zi)
A  Real(Z0)
B  Imag(Z0)
C  Expression in Constant Text Fields
